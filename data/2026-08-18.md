### [1] [THRIVE: Therapeutic Humanoid Robot In Virtual Environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14462)
*Jin Xu,Yu-Ping Chen,Ayanna Howard*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: THRIVE是一套面向上肢运动功能障碍儿童的居家康复平台，融合虚拟现实训练游戏、摄像头动作追踪和具备社交交互能力的机器人治疗师，以提供持续、个性化和富有参与感的康复训练。

Motivation: 儿童上肢运动康复需要持续且具有任务针对性的练习，但传统治疗在居家场景下面临训练依从性、反馈及时性、成本和可扩展性等问题。因此，研究旨在构建一种能够提升训练参与度、提供实时动作指导并适用于家庭环境的康复系统。

Method: 系统由三部分组成：面向功能性伸手、抓握和物体操作的虚拟现实游戏；基于摄像头的实时运动追踪模块，用于采集儿童训练过程中的运动学表现；以及机器人治疗师，可通过实体机器人或远程呈现的虚拟代理提供动态、自适应反馈。平台将治疗游戏与机器人形态解耦，从而支持不同机器人和交互载体。

Result: 研究提出并实现了包含多类训练任务的THRIVE平台，包括弹出、击打、接取和抓取等交互形式，可根据治疗目标进行定制。系统能够在游戏过程中追踪动作表现，并由机器人治疗师提供激励和运动引导。机器人无关的模块化架构增强了平台在不同设备和家庭场景中的适配性、可扩展性与潜在可负担性。

Conclusion: THRIVE为儿童居家上肢康复提供了一种融合沉浸式训练、动作量化和社交机器人反馈的系统方案。其模块化、机器人无关设计有助于降低部署成本并支持长期使用，但摘要未报告临床试验、疗效指标或与现有康复方法的对照结果，因此实际治疗效果仍需进一步验证。

Abstract: This paper presents THRIVE (Therapeutic Humanoid Robot In Virtual Environment), an at-home rehabilitation platform that integrates a suite of virtual-reality upper-body rehabilitation games, a real-time camera-based motion-tracking system, and a socially interactive robot therapist. The system is designed for therapy and intervention in children with upper-limb motor impairments, which can be improved through consistent, task-specific practice. THRIVE features a set of newly designed, engaging games that target functional reaching, grasping, and object-manipulation movements through customizable popping, hitting, catching, and grabbing tasks, while the camera-based tracking system captures the child's kinematic performance during play. A robot therapist - deployable either as a physical robotic coach or as a remote-presence virtual agent - delivers adaptive, dynamic feedback to motivate the child and guide their movements toward therapeutic goals. THRIVE decouples the therapeutic games from the robot embodiment, extending the platform to support various embodiments and different robots within one modular system. This robot-agnostic design makes THRIVE affordable, scalable, and readily adaptable for sustained use in the home, offering a practical pathway to more consistent and engaging upper-limb therapy for children with motor function impairments.
